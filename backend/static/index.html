    Persistent subtask atoms — emitted at a timestamp and remain in effect until the next subtask is emitted. Pi0.7 style: capture how, not just what (e.g. "grasp the handle of the sponge with the left hand").

    Persistent plan atoms — high-level decomposition of the task. The plan is typically emitted at t=0 and re-emitted on each user interjection. Multiple lines are kept verbatim.

    Persistent memory atoms — compressed semantic state. Emit at subtask boundaries. Keep only information relevant for remaining steps; compress completed steps; drop how, keep what; shorter is better.

    Event atoms in language_events — exact-frame timestamps. A user interjection is paired with an assistant speech tool-call (say); either or both can be emitted. The writer in lerobot#3471 enforces that every interjection has a paired speech atom on the same frame.

    Event vqa atoms — exact-frame question/answer pairs. Use the video canvas above to draw bounding boxes (drag) or points (single click). The assistant answer is JSON-encoded into content, matching the schema in the steerable pipeline writer.
